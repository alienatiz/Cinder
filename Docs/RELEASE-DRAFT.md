# Cinder

**Audio burn-in, made simple.**

Cinder is an audio burn-in app for earphones and headphones. Choose pink noise,
music, or a frequency sweep and start a burn-in session that fits your needs.
Set playback and rest times, follow progress from the menu bar, and keep your
burn-in history in one place.

## Burn-in on your schedule

Run a 40-hour plan in one session or split it into sessions with your choice of
rest intervals. You can also set a custom duration. Check remaining time and the
estimated finish in one view, and pause or stop whenever you need to.

## Choose your burn-in signals

Use pink noise, band-limited noise, a frequency sweep, or one of seven built-in
music tracks. Add music from your Mac and choose its order, or use the full cycle
to play a sequence of signals and rests.

## Keep control in the menu bar

Choose an output and duration in Quick Play Lite and start your session.
Burn-in continues when you close the window. While you work in other apps,
check the remaining time, pause, or resume from the menu bar.

## See your burn-in history

Review playback and rest times and how each session ended. Search records, copy
a summary, or export it to a file. History stays on this Mac, and basic playback
requires no account or internet connection.

macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon · English, Korean, and Japanese
